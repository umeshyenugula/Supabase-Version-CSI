CSI GRIET is the official student chapter of the Computer Society of India at Gokaraju Rangaraju Institute of Engineering and Technology.

Who We Are

A community that codes, creates & leads.

Founded as the GRIET chapter of the Computer Society of India, we've been at the forefront of technical education and innovation for over eight years.

We believe that the best engineers are shaped not just in classrooms but in collaborative, high-energy environments where curiosity meets execution.

Our Vision

To be the most impactful technical community in Telangana's engineering education.
