Vision

Our group aims to develop skills among students in all aspects. GRIET CSISB spotlights the latest and imminent technology from across the globe, committed to the advancement of research, development, training and education under CSI.

About Us

Formed in 1965, CSI is the first and largest body of computer professionals in India. GRIET CSISB Student Chapter, established in 2014, works to acquaint students with the latest technology — now with 500+ student members.

Mission

CSI Student Chapter organises events which are a collection of knowledge, learning and fun-filled technical and non-technical activities. With innovative projects and enlightening current trends, social responsibility is built among students.
